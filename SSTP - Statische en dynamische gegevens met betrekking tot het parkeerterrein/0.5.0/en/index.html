Introduction to semantic modelling

A concept scheme (in SKOS: ConceptScheme) is a structured collection of concepts that carry a fixed, shared meaning within a particular domain. The goal is for everyone working with the model — people and systems alike — to use the same words for the same things. That sounds obvious, but in practice language causes a surprising amount of confusion: what one organisation calls a measurement point, another calls a sensor, a location, or an observation station.

A concept scheme solves this by recording, for each concept:

  • a preferred label (prefLabel) it is known by,
  • a definition that unambiguously describes what the concept does and does not cover,
  • and optionally a scope note or example to clarify borderline cases.

Concepts rarely stand on their own. They are related hierarchically (a vehicle is broader than a truck) or associatively (a road user relates to a road segment). These relationships make it possible for queries and analyses to navigate between general and specific concepts, and to make connections between concepts explicit. Within SKOS we use broader, narrower, and related for that.

When a concept also exists elsewhere — in a national or European standard, for instance — we record that as a mapping relation. This prevents reinventing the wheel: we link to the source so interoperability with external systems remains possible.

This document describes the concept scheme as it is currently recorded. The following sections list the metadata, every concept, the relationships between them, and — where the model is connected to a data source — how it is linked to that source data.

Concepts

User

Concept details
FieldValue
DefinitionA person or body that makes use of the truck parking area or of the information about it.
ScopeAny truck or commercial vehicle driver, dispatcher, haulier, traffic management body, or any other body such as cargo owners, insurance companies, road authorities and police forces. They should obtain the information from the service providers.

User Feedback

Concept details
FieldValue
DefinitionThe response from a user about a truck parking area or about the information provided on it, such as a rating, a comment or a report of incorrect data.
ScopeUser feedback may be given anonymously. The feedback can be used to improve the quality of the published static and dynamic data.
ExampleFictitious example — a driver anonymously reports via an app that the published number of available spaces at Truckparking Voorbeeldstad does not match the actual occupancy.

Status

Concept details
FieldValue
DefinitionThe current operational condition of a truck parking area, indicating whether parking spaces are available, whether the area is almost full or full, or whether it is closed.

DefinitionA notification of a circumstance resulting in a truck parking area being wholly or partly out of use, such as maintenance, an incident or an emergency, including its reason.

Occupancy Rate

Concept details
FieldValue
DefinitionThe current percentage of the total truck parking capacity at a truck parking area that is occupied.

Entry Waiting Time

Concept details
FieldValue
DefinitionThe estimated time a vehicle has to wait at the entrance or access control of a truck parking area before it can enter.

Motorway Exit

Concept details
FieldValue
DefinitionThe exit or exits that a truck driver must take to reach the truck parking area from the primary road or to leave the area.
ScopeThe exit is indicated by the number, location code and/or name of the junction or interchange. In the Netherlands, motorway exit numbers are assigned by Rijkswaterstaat and published in the NWB.
ExampleFictitious example — exit 99 'Voorbeeldstad' on the A99.

Distance from Primary Road

Concept details
FieldValue
DefinitionThe distance between the exit to be taken and the entry point of the truck parking area.
ScopePreferably expressed as the travel distance by road in metres, not as the straight-line distance. For truck parking areas not located along a motorway, the road of the highest order in the vicinity is regarded as the primary road; this data item may then be optional.
ExampleFictitious example — travel distance from exit 99 to the entry point: 850 m.

Security Level

Concept details
FieldValue
DefinitionThe tier of security certification achieved by a truck parking area under the European classification framework established by Delegated Regulation (EU) 2022/1012, or an equivalent national classification system. The four European tiers are Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Platinum.
ScopeBronze requires basic perimeter fencing and lighting. Silver adds CCTV coverage. Gold additionally requires 24/7 staffed or remote surveillance and a parking management system with DATEX II output capability. Platinum requires all Gold features plus active threat monitoring and an integrated security operations centre. Certification is performed by accredited conformity assessment bodies under EN ISO/IEC 17021-1. In the Netherlands, the SecureNL consortium is a relevant certification body.
ExampleGold level: 24/7 remote CCTV surveillance, automated entry/exit barriers, DATEX II data feed to NDW, minimum 15 Lux lighting at ground level, perimeter fencing ≥ 1.8 m.

Security Feature

Concept details
FieldValue
DefinitionA physical or technological measure installed at a truck parking area to prevent theft, cargo tampering, or unauthorised access.
ScopeDelegated Regulation 2022/1012 Annex I specifies minimum technical requirements per feature per tier (e.g. CCTV minimum HD 720p resolution at 5 fps for Silver and above).
ExampleCCTV covering all truck bays and entry/exit points, HD 720p minimum, 30-day recording retention; perimeter fencing 2.0 m galvanised steel mesh with anti-climb top rail.

Parking Capacity

Concept details
FieldValue
DefinitionThe total number of parking spaces designated for trucks and commercial vehicles at a parking area, as required by Delegated Regulation (EU) No 885/2013.
ScopeThe capacity figure refers exclusively to spaces dimensioned for articulated lorries (opleggers/semi-trailers) unless otherwise specified. Spaces for refrigerated goods vehicles are reported separately. The capacity can concern static and dynamic figures.
ExampleTotal truck spaces: 120 (of which 8 refrigerated). The static capacity remains 120 regardless of current occupancy.

Certification

Concept details
FieldValue
DefinitionThe formal attestation by an accredited conformity assessment body that a truck parking area meets the requirements of a specified security tier under Delegated Regulation (EU) 2022/1012, including the certification name, issuing body, and date of issue.
ScopeCertification is voluntary under Delegated Regulation 2022/1012 but enables the parking area to be included in the EU's publicly accessible list of certified SSTPAs. Certification bodies must be accredited under EN ISO/IEC 17021-1. In the Netherlands, ESPORG and the SecureNL consortium are active in this domain. Gold and Platinum certified parking areas are required to have a functioning DATEX II data connection to the national access point.
ExampleCertificate: 'SSTPA Gold', issued by SecureNL, certification date: 2024-03-15, valid until: 2027-03-14.

Operator

Concept details
FieldValue
DefinitionThe legal entity responsible for the day-to-day management and operation of a truck parking area, including the provision of contact information for publication, as required by Delegated Regulation (EU) No 885/2013.
ScopeThe operator's identity can be verified against the Dutch Chamber of Commerce (Kamer van Koophandel) registration. The information records whether the operator has consented to their contact details being made publicly available via the national access point. The operator may differ from the wegbeheerder (road authority), particularly at privately operated truck parking facilities.
ExampleFictitious example — operator: Voorbeeldparking B.V., Chamber of Commerce (KvK) number 00000000; consent to publish contact details: yes.

Service Facility

Concept details
FieldValue
DefinitionEquipment, amenities, or services available to drivers and vehicles at a truck parking area, beyond the basic parking provision, as required by Delegated Regulation (EU) No 885/2013.
ScopeThis covers approximately 60 equipment types including showers, toilets, restaurant, fuel station, AdBlue dispensers, electric vehicle charging points, weighbridge, and tyre inflation. For verzorgingsplaatsen on the national highway network, minimum facility requirements are set by the CROW KIVZP criteria as applied by Rijkswaterstaat. Facilities influence the commercial attractiveness of a truck parking area but are secondary to the mandatory static data fields.
ExampleShowers (8 cabins), toilets (24/7 accessible), restaurant (06:00–22:00), AdBlue dispenser, high-pressure truck wash, weighbridge, 8 refrigerated vehicle electrical connections (CEE 63A).

Truck Parking Area Identification Information

Concept details
FieldValue
DefinitionThe information by which a truck parking area can be unambiguously identified, comprising its name, its national identifier (if available) and its address, including the country and territorial unit in which it is located.
ScopeMaximum 200 characters per field. In the Netherlands, the address must be consistent with the BAG (Basisregistratie Adressen en Gebouwen) where a BAG address exists. If the area is located along a motorway without a street address, the address may be supplemented with the name or number of the road, the hectometre position and the direction of travel from which the area is accessible.
ExampleFictitious example — name: 'Truckparking Voorbeeldstad'; national identifier: NL-TP-0000; address: Fictieweg 1, 9999 ZZ Voorbeeldstad, the Netherlands.

Refrigerated Goods Vehicle Space

Concept details
FieldValue
DefinitionA parking space specifically designated and equipped for refrigerated goods vehicles (koelwagens), including provision for powered reefer units (stroomaansluitingen), reported separately from total truck capacity as required by Delegated Regulation (EU) No 885/2013.
ScopeThe number of spaces available for refrigerated goods vehicles in accordance with Delegated Regulation (EU) No 885/2013. Operators usually report this number based on installed electrical connection points (CEE 32A or CEE 63A sockets).
ExampleA parking area with 120 total truck spaces of which 8 are designated for refrigerated goods vehicles, each equipped with a CEE 63A connection for the reefer unit.

Operator Contact Details

Concept details
FieldValue
DefinitionThe information enabling users of a truck parking area to contact the parking operator or an associated company, comprising the telephone number and e-mail address of the contact point and, optionally, the name of the contact person.
ScopeThese details are only made public if the operator consents; Delegated Regulation (EU) No 885/2013 records that consent as a separate data item.
ExampleFictitious example — contact point: reception Truckparking Voorbeeldstad; telephone: +31 00 000 0000; e-mail: contact@voorbeeldparking.example.

Truck Parking Area

Concept details
FieldValue
DefinitionAn identifiable and designated, off-street area specifically intended for the overnight or rest-period parking of trucks and commercial vehicles, providing safe and secure conditions.
ScopeIn the Netherlands, a truck parking area is formally designated by a traffic order (verkeersbesluit), resulting in the placement of RVV 1990 sign E8a ('Parking for trucks and buses'). On the national road network (Rijkswaterstaat), truck parking is often integrated into motorway service areas (verzorgingsplaatsen). In addition, municipalities may designate truck parking areas.
ExampleA2 Truck Parking at Eindhoven (N2 corridor); a verzorgingsplaats along the A16 near Breda equipped with dedicated truck bays and security fencing.

Primary Road

Concept details
FieldValue
DefinitionThe identifier and travel direction of the road or roads (maximum two) from which the truck parking area is directly accessible.
ScopeRoad identifiers follow the Dutch national numbering convention: A-wegen (autosnelwegen), N-wegen (nationale wegen), and S-wegen (stadsroutes). Road identifiers are maintained in the NWB (Nationaal Wegenbestand). The direction field indicates the main destination served, enabling drivers to identify the relevant parking area from route guidance.
ExampleRoad: A2, direction: Den Haag; secondary road: N2, direction: Eindhoven-Centrum.

SSTP Data Type – Static and dynamic Parking Data

Concept details
FieldValue
DefinitionData type within the SSTP context that describes the collection of concepts required to unambiguously record and exchange static and dynamic information about safe and secure truck parking areas on the trans-European road network. Static information concerns the fixed characteristics of an area; dynamic information concerns its current state and status.
ScopeThis data type is defined as part of the Dutch implementation of the ITS Directive (2010/40/EU) and Delegated Regulation (EU) No 885/2013 on information services for safe and secure parking places for trucks and commercial vehicles. Static data must be made available via the national access point (NTM/NDW) and updated at least annually.

Tariff

Concept details
FieldValue
DefinitionThe parking fee and currency applicable at a truck parking area, or an indication that parking is free of charge, as required by Delegated Regulation (EU) No 885/2013.
ScopeTariff information is governed by private law between the parking operator and the user (exploitatieovereenkomst). There is no Dutch public law obligation for truck parking tariff levels. The tariff published through NTM/NDW must be updated whenever the commercial tariff changes; the general minimum update frequency of annually applies.
Example€ 30,00 per 24-hour period (EUR); or: free of charge (for short stops at verzorgingsplaatsen without a parking management system).

Operating Hours

Concept details
FieldValue
DefinitionThe periods during which a truck parking area is open and accessible to vehicles, including any restrictions by day of week or time of day. Relevant both to access conditions and to the validity of tariff information.
Scope24/7 availability is a prerequisite for Gold and Platinum certification under DR (EU) 2022/1012 Annex I. Operating hours may affect the reliability of real-time availability data: a closed parking area should be reflected in the dynamic data type as 'closed'. In DATEX II this maps to OpeningTimes within the ParkingRecord structure.
ExampleOpen 24/7 all year; or: Monday–Friday 06:00–22:00, Saturday 08:00–18:00, closed on Sundays and public holidays.

Entry Point

Concept details
FieldValue
DefinitionThe location of the point at which vehicles enter the truck parking area.
ScopeThe position may be expressed as geographic coordinates (ETRS89 recommended) or as a location reference along the road network (point or linear reference, e.g. via the NWB). A truck parking area may have more than one entry point.
ExampleFictitious example — coordinates: latitude 52.00000, longitude 5.00000 (ETRS89); or as a location reference: a point on an NWB road section of the N999.

Supervision

Concept details
FieldValue
DefinitionThe type of monitoring and supervision arrangement in place at a truck parking area, indicating whether the site is staffed, remotely monitored, or unattended.
ScopeDATEX II ParkingSupervisionEnum includes: patrolled, remoteSurveillance, staffOnSite, controlCentre, none. Gold and Platinum certification under DR 2022/1012 require at minimum remoteSurveillance with 24/7 coverage. Supervision type directly affects the real-time responsiveness to incidents and is relevant to the dynamic data type.
ExamplestaffOnSite: a security guard present at the site 24/7; remoteSurveillance: CCTV feeds monitored from a central control room in Amsterdam, with intervention capability via contracted response service.

Safety and Security Classification

Concept details
FieldValue
DefinitionA structured description of the security measures, safety provisions, and supervision arrangements present at a truck parking area, as required by Article 4(2)(a) of DR (EU) No 885/2013, including any applicable national or European certification classification. Maps to DATEX II SafetyStandardClassification.
ScopeThe classification encompasses three sub-dimensions: security features (beveiligingsvoorzieningen), safety provisions (veiligheidsvoorzieningen), and supervision type (toezicht). DR (EU) 2022/1012 Annex I defines the European four-tier certification framework (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum) that specifies minimum requirements per tier. Where a national classification system exists, it must be reported in the securityNationalClassification field.

Safety Provision

Concept details
FieldValue
DefinitionA facility at a truck parking area that serves to protect the physical safety of drivers and occupants, as distinct from cargo security measures.
ScopeThese are distinct from security features (beveiligingsvoorzieningen) which concern crime prevention.
ExampleEmergency telephone connected to a 24/7 manned control room; fire extinguisher posts every 50 metres along the parking rows; AED (defibrillator) at the reception building.

Traffic Order

Concept details
FieldValue
DefinitionA formal administrative decision by the competent road authority (wegbeheerder) designating a location as a truck parking area, establishing the legal basis for placing a E8a sign and enforcing access restrictions.
ScopeA verkeersbesluit for truck parking is taken. The procedure requires mandatory consultation with the police and publication in the Staatscourant, followed by a six-week objection and appeal period. Verkeersbesluiten are publicly accessible via officielebekendmakingen.nl and the LVBB. Not all truck parking areas have a dedicated verkeersbesluit — areas regulated under APV use an aanwijzingsbesluit instead.
ExampleTraffic order (verkeersbesluit) by Rijkswaterstaat, reference RWS-2021/12345, published in the Staatscourant on 14 April 2021: designation of service area X as a truck parking area with sign E8a.

Motorway Service Area

Concept details
FieldValue
DefinitionA rest area alongside a national highway (Rijkswaterstaat) providing at minimum a parking zone and sanitary facilities, and often incorporating designated truck parking bays. Managed by Rijkswaterstaat under the CROW KIVZP criteria.
ScopeVerzorgingsplaatsen are formally registered by Rijkswaterstaat and appear in the NDW truck parking register. They carry the E8a sign for the truck section and are bound by Rijkswaterstaat's concession or management agreements for service facilities. Not all verzorgingsplaatsen meet the security classification thresholds of Delegated Regulation (EU) 2022/1012.
ExampleVerzorgingsplaats De Lucht (A2, Noord-Brabant) — includes a separate fenced truck parking zone with sanitary facilities and a restaurant.

Vehicle Category

Concept details
FieldValue
DefinitionThe category of vehicles for which a parking area or specific parking spaces are designated, based on the physical and operational characteristics of the vehicle, as defined in the RVV 1990 and EU vehicle classification standards.
ScopeIn the context of DR 885/2013, the relevant vehicle categories are trucks (vrachtauto's), articulated lorries (opleggers), and buses (bussen). Access restrictions based on vehicle category are governed by RVV, which prohibits parking at an E4–E13 designated location for vehicles not belonging to the indicated category. Sign E8a specifically covers lorries and buses.
ExampleA parking area signed E8a may only be used by trucks (motor vehicles with a maximum authorised mass exceeding 3,500 kg) and buses. Passenger cars and vans up to 3,500 kg are excluded.

Road Authority

Concept details
FieldValue
DefinitionThe public body legally responsible for a road and any associated infrastructure, including truck parking areas on or adjacent to that road. The wegbeheerder is also the competent authority for issuing a verkeersbesluit.
ScopeAuthority is determined by WVW 1994 Article 18: Rijkswaterstaat for national highways (A-wegen and certain N-wegen), the provincial executive (Gedeputeerde Staten) for provincial roads, and the municipal executive (B&W) for municipal roads. On verzorgingsplaatsen, Rijkswaterstaat is both wegbeheerder and often concession grantor for the service operator.
ExampleRijkswaterstaat (for A2 Truck Parking along the A2); Gemeente Amsterdam (for a municipally designated truck parking area within city limits).
